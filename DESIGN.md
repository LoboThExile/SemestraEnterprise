# Design System: Semestra Enterprise

> Indie educational game studio preserving and celebrating Malaysian and Southeast Asian heritage.

## 1. Brand Identity & Character
- **Product:** Board games, educational card games, and interactive cultural experiences (flagship: *Malaysian Heritage Match*).
- **Audience:** Families, educators, tabletop gamers, and culture enthusiasts.
- **Mood:** Modern game studio aesthetic: dark, focused, polished, and premium. High visual contrast and tactile presentation.

## 2. Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY:** 2 (Balanced game studio presence with clear focal points)
- **RHYTHM:** 2 (Consistent grid layout with strong card hierarchy)
- **MOTION:** 1 (Crisp interactive states; 0.15s ease transitions; zero infinite pulses or loops)

## 3. Color Palette
- **Base Canvas:** `#0d0f12` (deep obsidian)
- **Surface / Cards:** `#16191f` (elevated studio slate)
- **Subtle Surface / Inset:** `#1c2028`
- **Primary Typography:** `#f1f5f9` (crisp white; contrast ratio > 16:1 against canvas)
- **Secondary Typography:** `#94a3b8` (cool mid-slate; contrast ratio > 7:1 against canvas, passes WCAG AA)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#262b36` (sharp structural border)
- **Brand Action Primary (Crimson/Coral):** `#d63031` (hover: `#b71c1c`, white text contrast: 4.85:1)
- **Brand Text Accent:** `#f87171` (vibrant coral for prices/highlights, contrast: 6.83:1 on dark)
- **Gold Accent (Featured Badge):** `#facc15` (warm amber gold)
- **Focus Ring:** `2px solid #f87171` with `2px` offset

## 4. Typography
- **Font Stack:** `"Segoe UI", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
- **Hierarchy:**
  - H1: 2.75rem (desktop) / 2rem (mobile), font-weight 700, `#f1f5f9`
  - H2: 1.75rem, font-weight 600, `#f1f5f9`
  - H3: 1.25rem, font-weight 600, `#f1f5f9`
  - Body: 1rem, line-height 1.6, `#94a3b8`

## 5. Craftsmanship & Anti-Slop Safeguards
- **Clean Structure:** No stacked glassmorphism blurs, no glowing cyan divider shadows, no infinite pulse scaling.
- **Accessible Navigation:** Semantic buttons, full keyboard operability, and high-contrast text across all screens.
- **Image Fitting:** Studio logo and cards preserve natural aspect ratios without distortion.
