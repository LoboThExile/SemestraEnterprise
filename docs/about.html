About Semestra Enterprise

Preserving and celebrating Malaysian and Southeast Asian heritage through interactive learning and educational games.

Our Mission

Semestra Enterprise is an indie educational publisher dedicated to making Southeast Asian cultural heritage accessible and engaging. We design tabletop games and interactive media that help students, families, and casual players discover and appreciate the region's rich traditions, folklore, and history.

Our Flagship Title

Our flagship educational card game, Malaysian Heritage Match, brings Malaysian culture to life through fast-paced matching and educational trivia. Designed for players of all ages, it introduces cultural motifs, traditional arts, foods, and landmarks in an approachable format.

Our Core Values

Cultural Appreciation

Highlighting the depth and diversity of Malaysian and Southeast Asian customs with authenticity and care.

Interactive Education

Creating learning tools where fun and knowledge reinforce each other naturally.

Universal Accessibility

Ensuring our games are easy to learn, affordable, and welcoming to learners of all backgrounds.

Craft & Quality

Committed to thoughtful design, balanced gameplay, and durable physical components.
