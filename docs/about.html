About Semestra Enterprise

Celebrating Malaysian cultural roots and youth entrepreneurship through engaging tabletop games and handcrafted gifts.

Our Story & Mission

Semestra Enterprise is a youth-led student company founded by passionate students from SMK Pantai Sepang Putra under the prestigious Junior Achievement Malaysia (JA Malaysia) Young Enterprise Program 2026. Our team came together with a unified goal: to create engaging products that celebrate Malaysian heritage and showcase student craftsmanship and innovation.

Our flagship creation, Malaysian Heritage Match (RM 55), is an educational tabletop card game designed to make learning about Malaysian culture, history, state traditions, royal costumes, foods, and landmarks exciting and interactive for families and classrooms.

Alongside our flagship game, our student entrepreneurs handcraft everlasting PipeCleaner Flower Pots (RM 15), floral Handmade Hairclips (RM 4), and retro Pixel-Art Keychains (RM 4). All products are available on our official JA Malaysia Mall storefront.

Guiding Values

What Drives Semestra Enterprise

Cultural Pride

Inspiring genuine curiosity and appreciation for Malaysia's multi-ethnic traditions, royal attire, and regional heritage.

Hands-On Learning

Designing games that seamlessly integrate classroom-friendly Malaysian cultural education into family game nights.

Youth Leadership

Practicing real-world enterprise operations, craftsmanship, financial literacy, and teamwork through JA Malaysia.

Community Connection

Creating wholesome, screen-free opportunities for friends, families, and students to connect and learn together.
