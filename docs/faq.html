Products & Gameplay

About Malaysian Heritage Match & Crafts

Malaysian Heritage Match is our flagship educational card game priced at RM 55. It highlights diverse Malaysian state cultures, royal traditional costumes (such as Baju Kurung Teluk Belanga, Cekak Musang), regional delicacies (Laksa Johor, Laksa Kedah), historical landmarks, and state flags. It is suitable for 2–6 players aged 6 and above.
Yes! The card game and accompanying factsheets feature bilingual descriptions in both Bahasa Melayu and English, making it accessible for classrooms, language societies, and diverse family households.
Along with our card game, our student entrepreneurs handcraft:
  • PipeCleaner Flower Pots (RM 15): Everlasting chenille sunflowers in mini ceramic pots. 100% maintenance-free.
  • Handmade Hairclips (RM 4 / 50% Off): Floral chenille clips with sturdy, non-slip grip.
  • Pixel-Art Character Keychains (RM 4 / 50% Off): Heat-fused perler bead keychains featuring Judy Hopps, Nick Wilde, Tulip, and Apple motifs.
Yes, 100%! Every single flower pot, hairclip, and keychain is hand-shaped, assembled, and quality-inspected by the student team of SMK Pantai Sepang Putra under the JA Malaysia Young Enterprise Program 2026.
Ordering & Payments
