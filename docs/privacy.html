Privacy Policy

Our commitment to transparent data handling, student privacy, and responsible web practices.

Last Updated: August 2026 | Applicable to Semestra Enterprise (SMK Pantai Sepang Putra)

1. About Us & Scope

Semestra Enterprise is a youth-led educational enterprise operated by students of SMK Pantai Sepang Putra under the guidance of Junior Achievement Malaysia (JA Malaysia) for the Young Enterprise Program 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how our website collects, uses, and safeguards information when you browse our website, explore our creations, or submit inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

As an educational student company, we adhere to strict data-minimization principles:

  • Direct Contact Information: When you send an order inquiry via our contact form, email, or WhatsApp, you may provide your name, school/organization, phone number, and email address. This information is strictly used to reply to your question or fulfill your order.
  • Zero Tracking or Ads: We do not run third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking pixels, or sell/monetize visitor data in any form.

3. Browser Local Storage & Theme Settings

Our website utilizes your browser's native Web Storage (`localStorage`) strictly for user experience preferences:

  • semestra_theme_preference: Saves your selected theme mode (System, Light Mode, or Dark Mode) so it stays applied when you navigate between pages.
  • semestra_storage_consent: Records whether you have accepted or opted out of storage preferences.

Opt-Out Right: You can click the Cookie Notice button in the footer at any time to opt out or clear saved preferences. Opting out immediately removes stored keys and sets the theme back to the system default.

4. Payment & Transaction Security

To ensure maximum safety for both customers and students:

  • We do not collect or store credit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking passwords on this website.
  • Official e-commerce purchases are conducted via the verified JA Malaysia Mall platform.
  • Direct orders are settled via manual DuitNow QR transfers where customers verify the transaction through their own banking app.

5. Student & Minor Privacy

Semestra Enterprise is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of all students, participating members, and minor visitors. No personal student records or sensitive identifying details are published or processed through this static storefront.
